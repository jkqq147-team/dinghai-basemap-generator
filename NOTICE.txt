DingHai Seamap overview and cells / seamap-cell-3

The global overview (land, water) and every cell layer except depth
(seamark, land, water, wetland, sea_area, landuse, roads, peaks, places)
are compiled directly from one OpenStreetMap snapshot
and OSM Data's land polygons (OpenStreetMap coastline processing):
Copyright OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License 1.0
(Licenses/ODbL-1.0.txt, official plain text, opendatacommons.org, retrieved
2026-10-25; https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). This repository
publishes the method used to derive the cells (ODbL 4.6); each release
record (sources in manifest.json and releases/{release}.json) records the
URL, date, bytes and SHA-256 of the OSM and land-polygon inputs, and the
grid name, DOI, bytes and SHA-256 of the GEBCO input. Each cell's own
metadata carries attribution, licence and the depth notice, not source
provenance. No third-party compiled Seamap or Protomaps archive, feature
or attribution is read or included. Planetiler is an Apache-2.0 build
dependency, not incorporated into the data; its pinned version and the
custom LGPL-3.0-only schema are in planetiler/.

Depth layer: GEBCO Bathymetric Compilation Group 2026 (2026). The
GEBCO_2026 Grid - a continuous terrain model for oceans and land at 15
arc-second intervals. doi:10.5285/4f68d5c7-45eb-f999-e063-7086abc036fa.
The GEBCO Grid is placed in the public domain; terms of use in
Licenses/GEBCO-terms-of-use.txt (https://www.gebco.net/data-products/
gridded-bathymetry/terms-of-use, retrieved 2026-10-25). No official status
or GEBCO/IHO/IOC endorsement is implied. Its offshore distance uses the
same OSM land polygons as the land layer.

The depth bands are illustrative: interpolated from the GEBCO grid, not a
survey, not for navigation (示意、非测量、不可用于导航). These are general
map data, not an ENC or navigation chart.

tests/fixtures/gebco-fractional.nc is a small GEBCO-format test grid, under
the GEBCO terms above. depth_product.py, seamap_product.py, audit_seamap.py, land_subset.py,
satellite_product.py, satellite_overview.py, satellite_mosaics.py, audit_satellite.py,
planetiler/ and tests/ are offered under LGPL-3.0-only; the GNU LGPLv3 and
the GPLv3 it incorporates are in Licenses/LGPL-3.0.txt and Licenses/GPL-3.0.txt.
